Dataset description

In this project, we first analyse the growth-coupled sensitivity of the lac operon of E. coli to TMG, in the presence or not of a transient growth arrest. The corresponding dataset contains the following experiments:

We next analyse the growth-coupled sensitivity of the lac operon to lactose when E. coli grows on mixtures of glucose and lactose, both at the population level with different lactose concentrations and at the single-cell level for a given lactose concentration.
